CONTACT ZAMMIOHUB

We're here to help.

Have a question, found a calculator issue or have an idea that could make ZammioHub better? We'd be glad to hear from you.

Get in Touch

For website-related questions, feedback, calculator reports and general enquiries, you can contact ZammioHub directly by email.

Email ZammioHub

Send us an email with a clear subject line and enough information for us to understand your question or report.

contact@zammiohub.com

Your Privacy Matters

Please do not include passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, medical records or other unnecessary sensitive personal information in your email. For more information, see our Privacy Policy.

What Can We Help With?

Providing a little context in your email can help us understand your message more clearly.

Report a Calculator Issue

If a calculator is not working as expected, tell us the calculator name, the values you entered and what happened. This helps us investigate the issue.

Suggest a Calculator

Have an idea for a useful calculator that is not currently available? Send us the name of the tool and what you would like it to calculate.

General Questions & Feedback

You can contact us with general questions about ZammioHub, usability feedback or suggestions that may help improve the website.

Privacy & Website Enquiries

For questions concerning our privacy practices, website policies or other site-related matters, email us with a clear description of your enquiry.

Help Us Understand Your Message

When reporting a problem, a few useful details can make it easier to understand what you experienced.

Name the calculator or page

Tell us which ZammioHub calculator or website page your message relates to.

Describe the issue clearly

If something went wrong, explain what you expected and what you actually saw.

Include useful technical details

For display or functionality problems, mentioning your device and browser can be helpful.

About Responses

We review website-related messages and aim to respond when a response is appropriate. Response times can vary, and we cannot guarantee an immediate reply. ZammioHub does not provide individual medical, financial, tax or legal advice by email.
