Privacy-focused calculator experience

ZammioHub does not currently require users to create an account to use its calculators. Calculator values entered into our current browser-based tools are used to perform the requested calculation and are not used to create a personal ZammioHub profile.

1. Overview

ZammioHub provides online calculators and related informational content. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be handled when you use ZammioHub, why that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

By using ZammioHub, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Receive

Information you choose to provide

ZammioHub currently does not require an account or registration to use its calculators. However, if you contact us by email, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Your email address.
  • Your name, if you choose to provide it.
  • The content of your message.
  • Any other information you voluntarily include in your communication.

Please avoid sending passwords, payment card information, government identification numbers, medical records or other unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Technical information

Like most websites, basic technical information may be processed automatically by the services and infrastructure used to deliver and protect the website. This can include information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, timestamps and security-related request information.

3. Calculator Inputs & Results

ZammioHub's current calculators are designed to perform calculations using the values you enter. We do not currently require you to submit those values as part of a user account or personal profile.

You should nevertheless avoid entering unnecessary personally identifying or confidential information into calculator fields. Calculator results are intended for general informational or estimation purposes as explained further in our Disclaimer .

4. How We May Use Information

Information available to us may be used for legitimate website-related purposes, including to:

  • Operate, maintain and secure ZammioHub.
  • Respond to questions, feedback and calculator issue reports.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve website functionality.
  • Prevent abuse, malicious activity or security threats.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations when required.

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

ZammioHub does not currently use Google Analytics or Google AdSense on the website.

The website or the infrastructure used to deliver and secure it may use technically necessary cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, performance or abuse prevention.

If ZammioHub introduces analytics, advertising or other services that materially change how cookies or similar technologies are used, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

6. Service Providers & Third Parties

ZammioHub relies on third-party infrastructure and service providers to make the website available, route website traffic, provide security and support website-related communications. These providers may process limited technical information as necessary to provide their services.

We do not currently operate a system in which users create ZammioHub accounts containing calculator histories or personal profiles.

7. Links to Other Websites

ZammioHub may contain links to external websites or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of websites operated by other parties.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website you choose to visit.

8. Data Retention

Information you send to us directly, such as an email enquiry, may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, maintain relevant records, protect the website, resolve disputes or comply with applicable legal obligations.

We aim not to retain personal information longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is handled.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to support the security and integrity of ZammioHub. However, no website, network, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For this reason, users should avoid sending unnecessary confidential or highly sensitive information through email or website interactions.

10. Children's Privacy

ZammioHub is a general-purpose calculator website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through user accounts or profiles.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a direct communication and you would like to request its review or deletion, please contact us.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may provide certain rights concerning your personal information. These rights can vary by jurisdiction.

If you have contacted ZammioHub and would like to ask about personal information associated with that communication, you can contact us at the email address below. We may need to verify a request before acting on it where appropriate.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ZammioHub changes, including when new website features, analytics, advertising or other services are introduced.

When we make changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised. We encourage users to review this page periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or a privacy-related enquiry concerning ZammioHub, contact us at:

Email: contact@zammiohub.com

You can also visit our Contact Us page for general website enquiries.
