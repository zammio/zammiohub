How Does This Date Calculator Work?

Use the Date Calculator to find the number of days between two dates, or add and subtract a specific number of days from a starting date.

Calculate Days Between Two Dates

Enter a start date and an end date. The calculator shows the total number of calendar days, along with equivalent weeks, months, and years.

Add or Subtract Days, Weeks, Months, or Years

Choose a starting date, enter an amount, select Days, Weeks, Months, or Years, and choose Add or Subtract. The calculator gives you the resulting date and weekday.

Frequently Asked Questions About Date Calculations

How many days are between two dates?

Enter the start and end dates to calculate their calendar-day difference. You can also choose to include the end date in the result.

Can I calculate weeks between dates?

Yes. The result includes the equivalent number of weeks based on the calculated number of calendar days.

What is the difference between calendar days and business days?

Calendar days include every day between two dates, including weekends. Business days usually exclude weekends, and holidays may also be excluded depending on the calculation.

Can I add days to a date?

Yes. Use the Add / Subtract Days mode, enter a starting date and the number of days, and choose Add Days.

Can I subtract days from a date?

Yes. Select Subtract Days and enter the number of days you want to subtract from the starting date.

Calculate Business Days

Business days normally mean Monday through Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded, and you can enter custom public holidays to exclude them too.

Frequently Asked Questions About Date Calculations

How many days are between two dates?

Enter the start and end dates to calculate the calendar-day difference. You can optionally include the end date.

How do I calculate a date 30 days from today?

Use Add / Subtract, enter today as the starting date, choose 30 Days, and select Add.

How many business days are between two dates?

Use Business Days mode. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded, and you can enter additional holiday dates.

Does the calculator include public holidays?

Public holidays vary by country and year, so they are not assumed automatically. Enter the dates that apply to you.
