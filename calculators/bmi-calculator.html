What Is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening measure that compares your weight with your height. It is commonly used to identify general adult weight categories such as underweight, healthy weight, overweight, and obesity.

How Is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated using the formula BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²). This calculator automatically handles US and metric unit conversions.

BMI Categories for Adults

BMI Range Weight Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy Weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 and above Obesity

BMI Calculator in US and Metric Units

Use US Units for feet, inches, and pounds, or Metric Units for centimeters and kilograms. Other Units also supports meters, stone, and pounds.

BMI for Men and Women

Standard adult BMI categories are generally the same for men and women. However, BMI does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass, or fat distribution.

BMI for Children and Teenagers

For children and teenagers aged 2–19, BMI should be interpreted using age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age percentiles rather than adult BMI categories.

Is BMI Accurate?

BMI is a useful screening measure, but it does not directly measure body fat and should not be used as a diagnosis. Your BMI result should be considered together with other health information.

Frequently Asked Questions About BMI

How do I calculate my BMI?

Divide your weight in kilograms by the square of your height in meters. You can also use the calculator above to calculate BMI automatically.

What is a normal BMI?

For most adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly classified as the healthy-weight range.

Can I calculate BMI using pounds and feet?

Yes. Select US Units and enter your height in feet and inches and your weight in pounds.

Does BMI tell me my body fat percentage?

No. BMI is based on height and weight and does not directly measure body fat percentage.
