What Is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator is an online tool that helps you quickly find the discount amount, the money you save, and the final price after a percentage discount. It is useful for shopping, sale prices, online deals, retail offers, and everyday price calculations.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100
Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example: A 20% discount on 5,000 saves 1,000, making the final sale price 4,000.

How Does This Discount Calculator Work?

Enter the original price and the discount percentage. The calculator instantly shows the discount amount, your total savings, and the final price you need to pay after the discount.

What Is 20% Off a Price?

A 20% discount means you save 20% of the original price and pay the remaining 80%. For example, if an item costs 5,000, a 20% discount saves 1,000 and the sale price becomes 4,000.

Discount Calculator for Sale Prices

Use this free discount calculator whenever you see offers such as 10% off, 20% off, 30% off, 50% off, or any other percentage discount. It can help you compare sale prices and understand exactly how much money you will save.

How to Find the Final Price After a Discount

The final price after a discount is found by subtracting the discount amount from the original price. Our calculator performs both steps automatically and displays the final sale price clearly.

Discount Calculation Example

Suppose an item has an original price of 10,000 and a discount of 25%:

  • Discount amount = 10,000 × 25 ÷ 100 = 2,500
  • Money saved = 2,500
  • Final price = 10,000 − 2,500 = 7,500

Frequently Asked Questions About Discounts

How do I calculate a discount percentage?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100. The result is the discount amount you save.

How do you calculate 20% off a price?

Multiply the original price by 20 and divide by 100. Subtract that discount amount from the original price to get the final price.

How do I calculate the sale price after a discount?

First calculate the discount amount, then subtract it from the original price. The remaining amount is the sale price.

How do I find the original price after a discount?

This calculator works from the original price to the discounted price. If you know the sale price and discount percentage and want to reverse the calculation, a dedicated original-price calculator can be used.

What does “percent off” mean?

“Percent off” is another common way of describing a percentage discount. For example, 30% off means the customer pays 70% of the original price.
