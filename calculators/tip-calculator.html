How Does This Tip Calculator Work?

Enter the bill amount, choose a tip percentage, and enter the number of people sharing the bill. This tip and split bill calculator shows the gratuity, total bill, tip per person, and total amount each person pays.

Tip Calculator Formula

Tip Amount = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100
Total Bill = Bill Amount + Tip Amount
Total Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

Frequently Asked Questions About Tips

How do I calculate a tip percentage?

Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage and divide by 100. For example, a 20% tip on a 50 bill is 10.

How do I split a bill with tip?

Calculate the tip, add it to the bill, and divide the total by the number of people sharing the bill.

What tip percentage should I use?

Tipping customs vary by country, service type, and personal preference. Use a quick percentage button or enter any custom tip percentage.

What if a service charge is already included?

Check your bill before adding an extra tip. If a service charge or gratuity is already included, an additional tip may not be necessary depending on local custom and your preference.
