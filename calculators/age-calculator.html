How Age Is Calculated

Age is calculated by comparing the calendar dates rather than simply dividing the total number of days by 365. This allows the result to account for different month lengths and leap years.

Age Calculation
Exact age = completed years + completed months + remaining days

Why Use an Exact Age Calculator?

An exact age calculator is useful when you need a precise age for birthdays, forms, applications, eligibility checks, personal records, or other date-based calculations.

Frequently Asked Questions About Age Calculation

How do I calculate my exact age from my date of birth?

Enter your date of birth and the date on which you want to know your age. The calculator determines the exact difference in completed years, months, and days.

Can this age calculator show age in years, months, and days?

Yes. The result gives your exact calendar age in years, months, and days instead of an approximate number of years.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The calculation uses actual calendar dates, including leap-year dates, to provide an accurate result.

Can I calculate my age on a past or future date?

Yes. You can choose the date on which you want the calculation to be performed, provided it is not earlier than your date of birth.
