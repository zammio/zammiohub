How Does the Age Calculator Work?

Enter your date of birth and the date you want to calculate your age on. The calculator determines your exact age by accounting for the difference in years, months and days between the two dates.

Why Use an Exact Age Calculator?

A simple subtraction of birth years does not always give your exact age. This calculator considers the actual months and days between the dates, making it useful for birthdays, applications, forms, eligibility checks and everyday calculations.
