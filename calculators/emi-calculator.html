How to Calculate EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount a borrower generally pays every month toward a loan.

The EMI depends on three main factors: the loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan tenure.

EMI Formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

Where:
P = Loan amount
r = Monthly interest rate
n = Number of monthly payments

Example: If you borrow 500,000 at an annual interest rate of 10% for 5 years, the calculator will determine your monthly EMI, total interest, and total repayment amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the amount paid by a borrower each month to repay a loan over a specific period.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the loan amount, monthly interest rate, and total number of monthly payments. The standard EMI formula is used to calculate the monthly installment.

What information do I need to calculate EMI?

You need three basic details: the loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan tenure.

Can I calculate EMI for different loan tenures?

Yes. You can enter the loan tenure in either years or months to calculate the corresponding monthly EMI.

Does a longer loan tenure reduce EMI?

A longer loan tenure generally reduces the monthly EMI, but it can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.
