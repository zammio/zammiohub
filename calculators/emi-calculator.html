How Does This EMI Calculator Work?

Enter your loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan tenure. The calculator estimates your Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI), total interest payable, and total amount paid over the loan period.

EMI Formula

Monthly EMI Formula
EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ [(1 + r)ⁿ − 1]

Here, P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Why Use an EMI Calculator?

An EMI calculator helps you estimate monthly loan payments before comparing borrowing options, planning a budget, or evaluating the overall cost of a loan.

Frequently Asked Questions About EMI

What is included in the total loan payment?

Total loan payment includes the original loan amount plus the total interest payable over the selected loan tenure.

Does a longer loan tenure reduce monthly EMI?

A longer loan tenure usually reduces the monthly EMI, but it can increase the total interest paid over the full loan period.

How does the interest rate affect EMI?

A higher interest rate generally increases the monthly EMI and total interest cost, while a lower interest rate can reduce the overall borrowing cost.

Can I calculate EMI using years or months?

Yes. You can enter the loan tenure in either years or months. The calculator converts the tenure into monthly payments and calculates the corresponding EMI.
