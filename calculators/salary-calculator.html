What Does This Salary Calculator Calculate?

This tool is designed for gross salary conversion and compensation analysis. It can estimate regular pay, overtime earnings, additional cash compensation, employer benefits, total compensation and an effective hourly rate based on the assumptions you provide.

Hourly to Annual Salary

For a standard hourly employee, annual salary is estimated as hourly pay × hours per week × paid weeks per year. For example, $25/hour × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000 annual gross pay.

Annual to Hourly Salary

Annual salary is divided by annual working hours. Annual working hours are based on hours per week × paid weeks per year. This makes the result depend on your actual schedule rather than assuming every job has identical hours.

Biweekly vs Semimonthly Pay

Biweekly: annual salary ÷ 26 pay periods
Semimonthly: annual salary ÷ 24 pay periods
Quarterly: annual salary ÷ 4

Biweekly pay occurs every two weeks, while semimonthly pay occurs twice per month. They therefore produce different paycheck amounts even when the annual salary is identical.

Gross Salary vs Net Salary

Gross salary is pay before employee taxes and deductions. Net or take-home pay is what remains after applicable deductions. This calculator does not assume a country-specific tax system, so it should not be treated as a tax or paycheck calculator.

Overtime, PTO, Bonuses and Total Compensation

The advanced section lets you model additional compensation and time-off assumptions. Overtime is calculated from the regular hourly equivalent and the multiplier you enter. Bonuses and commissions are added to annual cash compensation. Employer retirement, health and other benefits are shown as part of total compensation but are not treated as cash salary.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate annual salary from hourly pay?

Multiply hourly pay by hours worked per week and paid weeks per year. Example: $25 × 40 × 52 = $52,000.

How do I convert monthly salary to annual salary?

Multiply monthly gross salary by 12.

How is biweekly salary different from semimonthly salary?

Biweekly pay generally has 26 pay periods per year, while semimonthly pay has 24. They are not interchangeable.

Does this calculator calculate taxes?

No. It focuses on gross salary and compensation. Taxes and deductions vary by country, region and personal circumstances.

What is an effective hourly rate?

It is an hourly-equivalent measure based on the annual cash compensation and the working hours represented by your assumptions. It can help compare jobs with different schedules.
