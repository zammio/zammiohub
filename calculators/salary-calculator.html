Salary Calculator Guide

How to Use This Salary Calculator

Enter your salary, choose the pay frequency and select a display currency. Enter your hours and workdays, then click Calculate Salary. Results update automatically as you change valid inputs. Open Advanced Options only when you need overtime, time off, bonuses, benefits or deductions.

What This Salary Calculator Calculates
  • Hourly, daily, weekly, biweekly, semimonthly, monthly, quarterly and annual equivalents.
  • Regular cash compensation plus overtime, double-time, bonuses, commission and other earnings.
  • Employer retirement, insurance, equity/RSU and other benefit values for total compensation.
  • Effective hourly value based on the actual working hours represented by your assumptions.
  • Optional generic deductions and estimated take-home pay.

Salary Calculation Guide

Hourly to Annual Salary

For a standard hourly schedule, annual gross pay is estimated from hourly pay × hours per week × paid working weeks. Unpaid full weeks and additional unpaid days reduce the working schedule.

Annual to Hourly Salary

The hourly equivalent is based on annual cash compensation divided by scheduled paid working hours. The effective hourly rate uses estimated hours actually worked after paid time off.

Biweekly vs Semimonthly Pay
Biweekly: annual amount ÷ 26 pay periods
Semimonthly: annual amount ÷ 24 pay periods
Quarterly: annual amount ÷ 4

Biweekly pay occurs every two weeks, while semimonthly pay occurs twice per month, so the periodic amounts differ even when annual compensation is the same.

Gross Salary vs Estimated Take-Home Pay

Gross salary is compensation before employee taxes and deductions. The optional take-home estimate uses the effective tax rate and deductions you enter; it does not apply country-specific tax brackets, credits, payroll rules or filing circumstances.

Overtime, PTO, Bonuses & Total Compensation

Overtime and double-time use the regular hourly equivalent and the multipliers you enter. Paid vacation, holidays and sick leave are paid time rather than extra salary. Bonuses, commissions and other cash earnings increase annual cash compensation. Employer benefits are shown separately and added to total compensation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does changing the currency convert my salary?

No. The currency selector changes the display symbol and currency code only. It does not perform foreign-exchange conversion. Enter the amount in the currency you selected.

How do I calculate annual salary from hourly pay?

Multiply hourly pay by hours per week and the paid working weeks represented by your schedule.

How do I convert monthly salary to annual salary?

Multiply monthly gross salary by 12 before any unpaid-leave adjustment that you choose to model.

What is the difference between biweekly and semimonthly?

Biweekly uses 26 pay periods per year, while semimonthly uses 24 pay periods.

Does paid vacation reduce my salary?

No. Paid vacation is already paid time. The calculator keeps it separate from salary and uses it when estimating hours actually worked and effective hourly value.

Does unpaid time change my salary result?

Yes, when you enter unpaid weeks or additional unpaid days, the calculator reduces the paid working schedule represented by the calculation. Avoid entering the same unpaid time twice.

Does this calculate country-specific taxes?

No. The optional deductions section is a generic estimate. Use a country-specific tax or paycheck calculator for jurisdiction-specific results.

What is total compensation?

Total compensation combines annual cash compensation with the employer benefit values you enter, such as retirement contributions, insurance, equity/RSUs and other benefits.

Can I compare two job offers?

Yes. Open the Compare Offers tab to compare base salary, bonuses, commission, benefits, PTO, working hours and effective hourly value using the assumptions you enter.
