How Does This Salary Calculator Work?

Enter your salary amount, choose your pay frequency, and adjust your work schedule if needed. The calculator converts your pay into equivalent hourly, daily, weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, monthly, and annual gross salary amounts.

How Is Salary Calculated?

Annual Salary = Hourly Pay × Hours per Week × Weeks per Year
Monthly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ 12
Bi-weekly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ 26
Semi-monthly Salary = Annual Salary ÷ 24

Gross Salary vs Net Salary

Gross salary is your pay before taxes and deductions. Net salary, or take-home pay, is the amount remaining after applicable taxes, benefits, and other deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions About Salary

How do I convert hourly pay to annual salary?

Multiply your hourly pay by the number of hours you work each week and then by the number of weeks you work each year.

How do I convert annual salary to hourly pay?

Divide your annual salary by your total working hours for the year. For example, 40 hours per week for 52 weeks equals 2,080 working hours.

What is the difference between bi-weekly and semi-monthly pay?

Bi-weekly pay is generally paid every two weeks, which usually means 26 pay periods per year. Semi-monthly pay is paid twice a month, which means 24 pay periods per year.

Does this salary calculator include taxes and deductions?

No. This calculator converts gross salary amounts before taxes and deductions. Your actual take-home pay can vary depending on taxes, benefits, and other deductions.
