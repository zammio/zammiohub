How Does This Loan Payment Calculator Work?

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan term. The calculator estimates the equal monthly payment, total interest, and total repayment for the selected term.

Loan Payment Formula

Payment = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ [(1 + r)ⁿ − 1]

P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments. For a 0% interest loan, the loan amount is divided by the number of months.

Frequently Asked Questions About Loan Payments

Does a longer loan term lower the monthly payment?

Usually, yes. A longer term spreads repayment over more months, which can lower the monthly payment but may increase the total interest paid.

Can this calculator handle a 0% interest loan?

Yes. Enter 0 as the annual interest rate. The calculator divides the loan amount equally across the selected number of monthly payments.

Does this loan calculator include fees or other charges?

No. It calculates principal and interest only. Lender fees, taxes, insurance, and other charges are not included unless they are already part of the loan amount you enter.
