How Does This Auto Loan Calculator Work?

Enter the vehicle price, down payment, trade-in value, estimated sales tax, fees, APR, and loan term. This auto loan and car payment calculator estimates your monthly car payment, amount financed, total interest, total loan payments, and year-by-year loan balance.

Auto Loan Payment Formula

M = P × [r(1 + r)n] ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

M is the monthly payment, P is the amount financed, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments. For a 0% APR loan, the amount financed is divided by the number of months.

What Affects Your Car Loan Payment?

Your monthly car payment depends mainly on the amount financed, APR, and loan term. A larger down payment or trade-in can reduce the amount borrowed. A higher APR generally increases the monthly payment and total interest, while a longer loan term can lower the monthly payment but may increase total interest.

Frequently Asked Questions About Auto Loans

How is a monthly car payment calculated?

A fixed-rate car payment is calculated using the amount financed, monthly interest rate, and total number of payments. This car loan calculator uses the standard amortization formula to estimate the monthly payment.

Does a larger down payment reduce my car payment?

Yes. A larger down payment reduces the amount you need to finance, which can lower your monthly payment and the total interest paid over the loan term.

Does a longer auto loan term lower the monthly payment?

Usually, yes. Spreading the loan over more months can reduce the monthly payment, but it may increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Does this calculator include sales tax and fees?

Yes. You can enter an estimated sales tax rate and optional financed fees. This calculator estimates sales tax using the full vehicle price. Actual tax, trade-in, registration, and fee rules vary by location and lender.
