How Does This Auto Loan Calculator Work?

Enter the vehicle price, down payment, trade-in value, estimated sales tax, fees, APR, and loan term. The calculator estimates your monthly payment, amount financed, total interest, and year-by-year balance.

Auto Loan Payment Formula

M = P × [r(1 + r)n] ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

M is the monthly payment, P is the amount financed, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments. For a 0% APR loan, the amount financed is divided by the number of months.

Frequently Asked Questions About Auto Loans

How is a monthly car payment calculated?

A fixed-rate payment is based on the amount financed, monthly interest rate, and number of payments using the standard amortization formula.

Does a larger down payment reduce my car payment?

Yes. A larger down payment reduces the amount financed, which can lower both your monthly payment and total interest.

Does this calculator include sales tax and fees?

Yes. You can enter an estimated sales tax rate and financed fees. Tax, trade-in, registration, and fee rules vary by location and lender.
